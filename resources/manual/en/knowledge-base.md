# Knowledge base

Articles about the things your desk is asked about most. Written once, read many times, and quoted into replies instead of retyped.

## Reading

Browse by category or search. Search looks at titles and bodies, so a phrase from the middle of an article finds it.

There are two kinds of article, and which you see depends on who you are:

- **Public** articles are visible in the portal to anybody who can raise a request.
- **Internal** articles are for the desk. Runbooks, the bit of the procedure that mentions the admin password location, notes that would alarm a customer.

The portal shows only the public ones. An article does not become public by accident — somebody chose it.

## On a ticket

The ticket page suggests articles that look relevant to what the ticket says. Suggestions are a starting point, not an answer: read one before you send it.

Attach the article that actually helped. That does two things — it tells the next person who opens this ticket what the answer was, and it builds a record of which articles are earning their place.

## Writing

If you can write articles, **Administration → Knowledge base** is where.

Some things worth knowing:

- An article is a **draft** until it is published. Drafts are invisible to everybody but their authors and editors, so there is no rush to get it right in one sitting.
- **Public or internal** is a decision to make deliberately, and it is the one worth checking twice before publishing. Moving an article from internal to public does not un-send what customers already read.
- Articles carry the same formatting as the rest of Ticktz, including pasted screenshots.
- Write the title as the question somebody would ask. "VPN disconnects every few minutes" gets found; "VPN troubleshooting (2024)" does not.
