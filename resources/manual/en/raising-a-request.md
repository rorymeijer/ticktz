# Raising a request

The portal is where you ask the desk for something. It is deliberately short: pick what you need, say what is wrong, and send it.

## Picking the right kind of request

The portal home shows the kinds of request your desk offers, grouped into categories. Each one is a different form, because "my laptop will not start" and "please give Anna access to the finance drive" need different information.

Pick the closest one. Nobody is going to send it back for being filed under the wrong heading — an agent can move it — but the closer you start, the fewer questions come back.

If you cannot find anything that fits, take the most general option available and describe the problem in your own words.

## Filling in the form

Every form has a **subject** and a **description**. Some have extra questions your desk has added, and those are the ones worth reading carefully: they are there because the answer is always needed, and giving it now saves a round trip.

A field marked with a red asterisk has to be filled in. Everything else is optional.

The description accepts formatting — headings, lists, bold, links — and you can paste a screenshot straight into it. A picture of the error message is almost always faster than describing it.

## Attachments

Add files with the paperclip. There is a size limit and a list of accepted types, both set by your desk; if a file is refused, the message says which of the two it fell foul of.

Attachments are visible to anybody who can see the ticket. Do not put passwords in them.

## What happens next

When you send the form:

- The request gets a **key**, like `SUP-1042`. That is its name for the rest of its life. Quote it if you phone someone about it.
- You are put on its **watcher** list, so you get an e-mail when something happens.
- It lands in a queue, and may be assigned to somebody automatically depending on how your desk is set up.
- If the kind of request you chose needs approval — a purchase, an access request — it goes to whoever has to approve it before any work starts. You will see that on the request itself.

You can reply to the e-mail you receive and your reply lands on the ticket. You do not have to come back to the portal to answer a question.
