# Getting started

Ticktz is a service desk: somewhere for people to ask your organisation for help, and somewhere for the people who answer to keep track of it.

## The two sides

Almost everything in Ticktz is one of two things.

**The portal** is where somebody asks. They pick the kind of request they need, fill in a short form, and then follow it as it is worked on. Most people who use Ticktz only ever see this side, and it is deliberately small.

**The agent console** is where the work happens. Tickets arrive there from the portal, from e-mail, and from whoever types one in, and they are sorted into queues, worked, and closed.

Which side you land on depends on what you are allowed to do, not on a setting. If you can work tickets, signing in takes you to the console; if you cannot, it takes you to the portal.

## Signing in

Your administrator either made you an account or connected Ticktz to your organisation's directory. If it is connected, your usual work password is the one that works here, and there is nothing separate to remember.

If you have forgotten a Ticktz password, **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in screen sends you a link. The link expires, so use it when it arrives rather than later.

## Finding your way

The navigation on the left shows only what you can reach. It is shorter for a customer than for an agent, and shorter for an agent than for an administrator, and that is working as intended — nothing is hidden from you that you could have used.

The **?** in the top bar opens the part of this manual that explains the screen you are on. It is the fastest way to use this manual: rather than coming here and looking for the right chapter, open the screen that is confusing you and press **?**.

## Your profile

**Profile**, under your name, is where you set:

- **Your language.** Ticktz speaks English and Dutch. This setting follows you between devices. You can also add `?lang=nl` or `?lang=en` to any address to see one page in the other language without changing anything.
- **Your signature.** If you reply to customers, this is added to the bottom of what you send by e-mail.
- **Your password**, when Ticktz holds it rather than your directory.

## What this manual covers

Every chapter here is about a part of Ticktz you can open. If a chapter you expect is missing, it is because the permission it needs is not one you hold — ask whoever administers your desk rather than assuming the feature is not there.
